Who handles your information

Above The Norm LLC is the business responsible for the customer information it uses through this storefront. In this policy, “ATN,” “we,” and “our” refer to Above The Norm LLC. This notice covers browsing this store, checkout and order support, email sign-up, restock requests, and business inquiries made through the website. Our mailing address and support email appear below.

Use this policy to understand what happens before entering information. A separate service, such as a card issuer, delivery carrier, or social platform, may have its own privacy notice for the information it handles. This notice concerns this ATN storefront and the related communications described here.

The categories below describe what this storefront uses. Which categories apply depends on the features you choose. Required checkout fields are used to identify your order and submit it for delivery and payment. Marketing sign-up and restock alerts are optional.

Where information comes from

ATN receives information directly from you when you enter checkout details, sign up for updates, request an alert, or contact us. The site also reads storefront preferences stored on your device to restore a saved bag or shipping choice.

For an order, the payment provider supplies transaction references and payment results. A carrier may supply tracking or delivery information for a shipment. If someone buys a gift for you, the buyer may supply the recipient’s name or address so the order can be delivered. Hosting services may process sign-in or network information needed to serve the site securely.

Shopping, checkout, and card data

Checkout asks for contact details and shipping and billing addresses. When you start the payment form, the storefront sends the information needed by the configured payment provider. The provider receives card details directly through its hosted payment form. ATN’s order system records the checkout email, order reference, items, shipping method, terms version, payment reference, and payment status or total where available. It also stores a fingerprint of the checkout details to recognize a retry without retaining a second copy of the addresses in the storefront order record.

Enter card number, expiration, and security code only in the connected provider’s form. ATN’s storefront does not collect those card fields in its own database. Use payment support for questions about an order and your provider or card issuer for questions about the card itself.

Cookies, browser storage, and site access

The storefront uses browser storage to remember your bag, shipping choice, welcome offer and whether the sign-up prompt has appeared. A temporary session record links a pending checkout to the right bag so it can be cleared after payment confirmation. These records support checkout and navigation on the device you use.

The hosting service may use session or sign-in technologies when access to the storefront requires an account. The payment provider may use cookies or similar technologies to operate checkout and check for misuse. To control storage, use your browser’s settings. Clearing site data may sign you out, clear your bag, and reset storefront preferences.

Marketing email and the welcome offer

Joining the ATN email list saves your email address and a record of your consent. The welcome offer links the sign-up email to eligibility for a 10% merchandise discount on an eligible first order. The dollar value to a shopper depends on the merchandise purchased. The discount excludes shipping and tax. See the offer terms for eligibility.

Signing up is optional. Use the unsubscribe link, when included in a marketing email, or contact support@abovethenorm.us to withdraw your marketing preference. Checkout verifies active sign-up when it applies the first-order offer. Unsubscribing can affect eligibility on a future checkout. ATN may keep a record of the opt-out to help honor it. Confirmations, delivery updates, and replies needed to service an order or request can still be sent. See the FTC’s commercial email guidance for more information.

Item and size alerts

A restock request records your email, the product and size requested, and the status of the request. The information is used to send the corresponding alert if that size becomes available and sending is enabled. A restock request is independent of joining the marketing list.

To cancel a request, contact ATN with the email address, product, and size. Where a cancellation link is provided, that link applies to the specific alert.

Support, order claims, and business inquiries

The Contact page prepares a draft in your email app. ATN receives the information you choose to send, such as your checkout email, order reference, the problem you describe, and pictures of the item or packaging. ATN uses this information to verify the order, handle the claim, and follow up. Avoid including card details, passwords, or documents unrelated to the request.

The partnership inquiry form stores contact and company information, the website if provided, and the details you submit. ATN uses the information to review the inquiry and respond. If it concerns an ongoing business relationship, we may use the contact details to manage the relationship or follow up about the proposal.

Who receives information

ATN makes information available to the services involved in the activity you choose and for the purposes described below.

  • Storefront hosting and site access. The hosting platform serves pages, holds operational records, and may process sign-in and network information.
  • Payment. The configured payment provider receives payment and related order information. The card networks and acquiring or issuing banks may process the transaction under their own privacy practices.
  • Fulfillment and carriers. Delivery services receive the destination, recipient information, and shipment details needed to deliver and track the package.
  • Email and customer support. Our email service, Microsoft 365, receives the address and message data needed to send the requested communication.
  • Required disclosures. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or as appropriate to protect the storefront, investigate suspected misuse, or resolve a dispute.

When you open a tracking result, the carrier receives the tracking number so it can show shipment scans. External social, carrier, or other links lead to services with their own privacy notices.

Sale, advertising, and browser opt-out signals

ATN does not sell personal information collected through this storefront, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for profiling to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. ATN uses the operational service providers described above to perform the corresponding tasks.

Global Privacy Control and other legally recognized opt-out preference signals communicate a browser preference about certain data uses. The storefront’s current practices exclude those advertising and sale uses regardless of the browser setting. You can also contact ATN to ask about your choices or submit an applicable opt-out request.

Retention and safeguards

ATN considers the reason information was collected, the services and support you requested, and applicable recordkeeping obligations when determining how long to keep it. Order and payment references may be needed for accounting, delivery, refunds, and disputes. Marketing records may be kept while a subscription is active, with consent or suppression information retained as needed to administer your preference. A restock record may be needed to send, cancel, or document the requested alert. An inquiry or request record may be kept long enough to respond and document the outcome.

The storefront uses HTTPS for checkout requests and the payment provider handles card entry. Owner tools that show order and subscription records require authorized access. Keep access to your checkout email and order reference private. Contact ATN if you think someone has used your information to place an order. Providers and carriers maintain some records under their own retention requirements.

Children’s information

This storefront is a general-audience clothing store. It is not directed to children under 13. ATN does not knowingly collect personal information online from a child under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child supplied personal information through this site, contact ATN so we can review the information and handle it under applicable law.

U.S. state privacy rights

Residents of all 50 states and Washington, D.C., can use the contact process below. Legal rights depend on state of residence, the kind of information and activity involved, and whether the applicable law covers the business or data. Other state consumer protection and privacy rules may apply even where there is no general consumer privacy statute.

  • Access or confirmation. Where provided by law, ask whether ATN processes your personal information and request a copy or a description of the information, sources, purposes, and disclosures.
  • Correction and deletion. Request correction of inaccurate information or deletion of covered information. Exceptions can apply to a transaction, a legal obligation, or a dispute.
  • Portability. Request a copy in a portable format when applicable and technically feasible.
  • Opt-outs and limits. Where recognized, opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or certain profiling, or request limits on certain uses of sensitive information.
  • Additional rights. Some laws provide information about third parties, special rules for automated decisions, appeal rights, or protection from discrimination or retaliation for exercising privacy rights.

California and other state-specific provisions

California. Where the California Consumer Privacy Act applies, California residents may have rights to know categories and specific pieces, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, limit certain uses or disclosures of sensitive personal information, and receive equal service and price when exercising applicable rights. The collection table identifies the categories gathered through this storefront and how they are used. The sale and advertising section describes current practices. The California Attorney General’s CCPA guide explains eligibility and request types.

Colorado and Connecticut. Applicable laws recognize access, correction, deletion, portability and certain opt-outs. They also provide for recognized opt-out signals for covered uses and a way to appeal certain decisions. See the Colorado Privacy Act resource and the Connecticut Attorney General’s privacy resource.

Minnesota and Oregon. Their privacy laws allow eligible consumers to ask for information about specific third parties that received personal data. Minnesota also provides additional rights concerning certain decisions based on profiling. See the Minnesota consumer rights statute and the Oregon Department of Justice privacy guidance.

Nevada. Nevada residents can send a verified covered-information sale opt-out request to support@abovethenorm.us under Nevada law where applicable. Use “Nevada privacy request” in the subject and identify the email address associated with the storefront. See the Nevada sale opt-out statute.

Rhode Island. Above The Norm LLC is the storefront controller for purposes of its website privacy disclosures. The categories of personal data collected are listed in Information collected and why. The storefront’s current practices involve no sales of customers’ personally identifiable information, so there are no sale recipients to identify. Use support@abovethenorm.us to contact the controller. See the Rhode Island Data Transparency and Privacy Protection Act.

Texas. Where the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act applies, qualifying consumers can submit requests for access, correction, deletion, a portable copy, or applicable opt-outs. See the Texas Attorney General’s guide for coverage and complaint options.

Other states. The common rights and request instructions on this page apply where your state’s law provides them. The directory below has a consumer protection contact for every state and D.C., including states with additional requirements for sensitive information, disclosures, opt-outs, or appeals.

How to submit and verify a privacy request

  1. Email support@abovethenorm.us with “Privacy request” in the subject, or use Contact ATN about privacy to open a draft. Send the request from the address associated with the order, sign-up, or alert if you can.
  2. Tell us your state of residence, right you want to exercise, and which information or feature the request concerns. Include an order reference only if it helps locate the record.
  3. ATN may ask for information reasonably needed to find the record, verify your identity or authority, and protect another person’s information. Keep full card numbers, security codes, and government identification out of the initial email.

An authorized agent can submit a request where applicable law permits it. Identify the consumer and include evidence of authority appropriate to the type of request. Depending on the law and request, ATN may ask the consumer to verify directly or confirm the agent’s authority.

ATN will respond within the timeframe applicable to a verified request. Some laws allow more time if the business gives notice and explains the extension. If part of a request falls under an exception or cannot be verified, ATN will explain the outcome where the law requires and provide applicable next steps.

Appeals and regulator complaints

If ATN declines a privacy request and your state provides an appeal right, reply to the decision or email support@abovethenorm.us with “Privacy appeal” in the subject. Include the original request date, the decision, and why you believe it should be reconsidered. ATN will respond to the appeal within the applicable timeframe and identify the regulator complaint option when required.

You can contact your state’s consumer protection office or privacy regulator using the directory below. California residents can also consult the California Privacy Protection Agency. Exercising a privacy right will not result in discrimination or retaliation prohibited by law.
