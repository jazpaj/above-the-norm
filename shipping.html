This policy explains how orders placed on this website are processed and delivered by Above The Norm LLC ("Above The Norm", "we", "us").

Processing time

Our pieces are printed and finished in small runs. Orders are produced, packed and dispatched within 7 to 14 business days of purchase (excluding weekends and U.S. federal holidays). Processing time for each item is also shown on its product page.

Shipping rates and delivery times

MethodCostTransit time
Standard$8, free on orders over $1503 to 7 business days after dispatch
Express$201 to 3 business days after dispatch

The shipping cost for your order is always shown at checkout before you pay.

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in all 50 U.S. states, Washington D.C. and APO/FPO addresses. We do not currently ship internationally. If you're outside the U.S., email us before ordering.

Order confirmation and tracking

You'll receive an order confirmation email right after purchase, and a second email with your tracking number when your order is handed to the carrier.

Delays, lost and stolen packages

Carrier delays are rare but can happen, especially around holidays. If your tracking hasn't updated for 7 days, or your package is marked delivered but you can't find it, email us at family@abovethenorm.us and we'll open a claim with the carrier and work with you on a replacement or refund.

Incorrect addresses

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email us within 24 hours. Packages returned to us because of an incorrect address can be reshipped once the customer covers the new shipping cost.
