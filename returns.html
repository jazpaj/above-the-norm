Customer care

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Because every piece is made in limited runs, all sales are final. We do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind or sizing, so please check the size guide before ordering. We stand behind every order, though, and the exceptions below always apply.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, has a manufacturing defect, or isn't the item, size or color you ordered, email family@abovethenorm.us within 7 days of delivery with:

  • your order number,
  • a description of the issue, and
  • a photo of the item and its packaging.

Once we confirm the issue, we'll send a replacement at no cost or, if a replacement isn't available, issue a full refund including original shipping. We may ask you to return the item; if so, we'll email you a prepaid return label.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order within 24 hours of placing it, as long as production hasn't started, by emailing us with your order number. Cancelled orders are refunded in full.

Lost packages

If a package is lost in transit, see our Shipping Policy. We'll file a carrier claim and send a replacement or refund once the carrier confirms the loss.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. We process them within 3 business days of approval; your bank or card issuer may take 5 to 10 business days to post the credit.

Questions

If you have any questions about an item before ordering, please contact us. We're happy to help with fit and sizing.
