Seller and scope

Above The Norm LLC, a Missouri limited liability company, operates the ATN storefront. In these terms, “ATN,” “we,” and “our” refer to Above The Norm LLC. These terms cover purchases from this storefront and explain how its shopping, tracking, and support features work. The business mailing address and support email appear at the end of this document.

Read these terms with our Shipping Policy and Returns & Refunds Policy. Those pages provide the details for delivery and order remedies. The Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled. A purchase from a different retailer is governed by the receipt and terms from that seller.

Who can place an order

Place an order only if you are legally able to do so where you live or have permission from an authorized parent or guardian. If you use someone else’s payment method, get the account holder’s permission first. Provide accurate contact, billing, and delivery information and keep access to the email address used at checkout.

The checkout is designed for delivery to the 50 U.S. states, Washington, D.C., and supported military addresses described in the Shipping Policy. Shipping eligibility depends on the delivery address. If you need delivery outside that area or a business purchase arrangement, contact ATN before submitting payment.

Product descriptions, sizing, and availability

Each product page identifies the piece, its color, sizes, and any details available for that listing. Fabric finish and garment color can appear different under different lighting, in photography, or on a screen. Review the product page for the details that apply to the piece you want.

The general size guide is a starting point. Open Size Chart on the product page to see its listed sizes and any product-specific fit notes. Compare measurements on a garment you already own. For a size outside the general chart or a measurement the listing does not specify, ask ATN for sizing help before ordering.

Adding a piece to your bag saves your selection on your device. Availability is checked when you proceed to payment. A size that is no longer available must be removed before completing checkout. When the product page offers a restock alert, you can request an email for that item and size.

Prices, shipping charges, and taxes

ATN displays prices in U.S. dollars. A price can vary by size or product option. The checkout summary shows merchandise, the discount you applied, and the shipping option you selected. Applicable taxes and the final amount are presented in the payment flow before you authorize payment.

Review the amount and currency shown in the payment form. If the details differ from what you intended, return to order review or contact ATN before paying. Your bank or card issuer may apply its own conversion or card charges under your agreement with it.

If an order is affected by a material listing or pricing error, ATN will contact you about the correction or cancellation as applicable. If ATN cancels an affected paid order, ATN will refund the amount paid for the canceled order.

Welcome offer and discount eligibility

ABOVE10 gives 10% off merchandise on your first online order when you use the email address from your ATN email sign-up. The offer applies before shipping charges and taxes. Apply it in your bag and confirm it appears in the checkout summary before paying. Use one code per order.

Standard shipping eligibility is based on the merchandise subtotal after discounts. The offer is subject to first-order eligibility and the code being available at checkout. Checkout verifies that the sign-up email is still active on the list. The Privacy Policy explains the information used to verify the offer and how to unsubscribe.

Review your order and accept the terms

  1. Review each item, color, size, and quantity in your bag.
  2. Enter your name, email, phone number, and complete delivery address. Include an apartment or unit if needed.
  3. Choose a shipping method and provide your billing address. Use the separate billing fields if your card uses a different address.
  4. Check the Terms of Service box to open the payment form. Review the final amount and authorize payment when ready.

Open the Terms of Service link beside the checkbox to read or save a copy before checking out. ATN records the terms version associated with checkout. The order summary, receipt, and confirmation together help identify what you agreed to purchase.

Payment, confirmation, and payment problems

Use the connected payment provider’s card form to authorize payment for the final amount shown. When you begin payment, ATN passes the order details needed by the provider. A saved bag, a checkout page, or a temporary authorization on your bank statement can appear before ATN has verified the payment.

ATN treats the order as confirmed after verifying successful payment. Keep the order reference and save your receipt. If payment is declined, review the billing details and contact your issuer if necessary. If the site times out, the order status remains pending, or you see a charge without a confirmation, contact ATN with the checkout email and reference. Let us check the existing attempt before starting a second payment.

For checkout help, use the payment support option. Keep card numbers, passwords, and security codes out of your message. For information about payment and order data, see the checkout privacy notice.

Order emails and service messages

Use an email address you can check after ordering. ATN or the payment provider may provide a receipt or confirmation. ATN may also use order contact details for delivery updates, shipping delays, payment questions, or support messages. If an expected message is missing, check spelling, spam, and Promotions before contacting us.

Joining the marketing list and requesting an item-specific restock alert are separate choices. Use the unsubscribe instructions in the Privacy Policy for marketing messages. Order and support communications can continue when needed for a transaction or request.

Shipping, delivery, and delayed orders

Orders normally ship within 7–14 business days after payment confirmation, excluding weekends and U.S. federal holidays. Preparation and carrier transit are separate. Standard U.S. shipping is $8 or free at $125 after discounts. Express is $20 and changes the transit estimate after dispatch. See the Shipping Policy for the current methods and timeframes.

If we cannot ship within the stated timeframe, we will notify you about the delay and your options to agree to the new shipping timeframe or cancel for a refund. Review the delay notice for instructions and contact support if you need help. The FTC’s online-order merchandise rule explains federal requirements for delayed shipments.

Use your shipping email to find the tracking number. The Track an Order page directs you to the carrier’s shipment scans. If tracking stops updating or a package is marked delivered and you cannot locate it, contact ATN using the steps in the Shipping Policy. Keep the order reference and tracking number available.

Address corrections and cancellations

Review the address on your order as soon as you receive confirmation. If a correction or cancellation is needed, contact ATN within 24 hours with the checkout email and order reference. For an address correction, include the corrected address. Once the carrier has the package, changes depend on the carrier.

ATN reviews cancellations before production starts. Approved cancellations are refunded in full. For exact steps and timing, see cancellations and refund timing. Cancellation rights for delayed or unshipped merchandise under applicable law remain available.

Returns, replacements, and refunds

Sales are final for change of mind or fit. If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact ATN within seven days of delivery with the order reference, affected item, a description, and photos of the item and packaging. Keep the product and packaging while we review the claim.

The Returns & Refunds Policy describes how ATN reviews a report, provides a prepaid label when an approved claim requires a return, and offers a replacement when available. If a replacement is unavailable for an approved claim, ATN provides a full refund including original shipping. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within three business days. Your bank or issuer may need another 5–10 business days to post the credit.

Those timeframes describe ATN’s ordinary process. Statutory deadlines, warranties, and remedies apply where the law provides them.

Product warranties and consumer protections

Federal and state laws can provide protections for inaccurate descriptions, undelivered or defective merchandise, or other consumer issues. Depending on the law that applies, you may have statutory or implied warranty rights and remedies. Keep the product listing, receipt, and communications relevant to your issue. If a product carries a separate written warranty, review the terms provided with that product.

Your rights and remedies that cannot be waived under applicable law remain available. The store’s final-sale language applies to voluntary returns for fit or change of mind. Where a law provides an additional remedy, the applicable law controls. See the FTC warranty guidance or your state consumer protection office for information.

Use of the storefront and its forms

Use the storefront to shop, track your own orders, and communicate with ATN. Use accurate information, respect another person’s information and account access, and submit requests for their intended purpose.

Prohibited conduct includes impersonating someone, using a payment method without authorization, attempting to access private order information, interfering with checkout or the storefront, submitting malicious files or code, or using the forms to harass people or send spam. ATN may restrict misuse of its forms or investigate a suspicious payment. Contact Customer Care if you believe access was restricted in error.

Payment, carrier, and external links

Some features use separate services. The payment provider handles the card form and authorization. The carrier handles the tracking view linked from the tracking page. When you use a third-party service, its terms and privacy information can also apply to that service.

Use ATN’s support contact for an order problem, even when the carrier or payment provider supplied an update. External links are provided for their specific purpose. A link to a social profile, historical reference, or carrier scan does not change which business sold the merchandise.

Governing law and all-state protections

To the extent permitted by law, Missouri law governs these terms and purchases from this storefront, without using its conflict-of-laws rules to select a different law. Applicable federal law applies as well. If a mandatory consumer protection, warranty rule, or other right in your home state or another applicable jurisdiction gives you protection that cannot be waived, that protection remains available.

This includes qualifying consumers across all 50 states and Washington, D.C. The choice of Missouri law does not take away any court, agency complaint, or other option that applicable law makes available. State rights can differ based on where you live and the specific transaction. See the Missouri Merchandising Practices Act and the consumer protection directory for state resources.

How to resolve an order dispute

If there is a problem, contact support@abovethenorm.us with the order reference, what happened, and what would resolve it. Keep copies of the receipt, tracking, photographs, and any relevant messages. If an issuer or carrier is involved, share relevant updates so ATN can identify the transaction or shipment.

You may seek help from your state consumer protection office or use a court or other complaint process available under applicable law. Small claims may be available when the matter qualifies under the local rules. Contacting ATN is an available first step.

Revisions and severability

The date at the top identifies this version. ATN may update these terms as the storefront, fulfillment, or applicable law changes. The version accepted when you place an order continues to govern that order, subject to any mandatory law or later agreement that applies.

If a provision cannot be applied under the law governing a particular matter, the other provisions continue to apply to the extent they can operate independently. Preserve a copy of the order terms and receipt for your records.
