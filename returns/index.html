Final sale and sizing

Sales are final for a change of mind or fit. Before ordering, compare the product’s size chart and fit notes with a garment you own. Use the general size guide or contact ATN for help with measurements. Your rights under applicable law still apply.

Report a damaged, defective or incorrect item

Contact ATN within seven days of delivery. Send the message from your checkout email and include the information below.

  1. Your order reference and the name and size of the item.
  2. A brief description of the damage, defect or difference from your order.
  3. Clear photos of the item and packaging. Include the shipping label if it helps identify the package.

Keep the piece and packaging while ATN reviews your claim. ATN will explain the next step and send a prepaid label if an approved claim requires a return.

Replacements and approved refunds

Approved claims are eligible for a replacement when one is available. If a replacement is unavailable, ATN issues a full refund including original shipping. If your package never arrived, see the tracking and delivery steps in the Shipping Policy.

Cancellation before production

If you need to cancel, request cancellation within 24 hours of placing the order. Include your order reference and checkout email. ATN reviews cancellations before production starts and refunds approved cancellations in full.

When a refund appears

ATN issues approved refunds to the original payment method within three business days. Your card issuer or bank may need another 5–10 business days to post the credit. Contact support@abovethenorm.us with your order reference if you need the refund status.
