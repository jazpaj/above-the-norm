Before You Order

Shipping, sizing, returns and more. Can't find it here? Email support@abovethenorm.us and someone on the team will answer in 1–2 business days.

All FAQs →
How long until my order ships?

Every piece is printed and finished in limited batches, so orders leave us within 7 to 14 business days. You'll get an email with tracking the moment your package is handed to the carrier.

What does shipping cost?

Standard U.S. delivery is $8, and it's on us once your order reaches $125 after discounts. Express is $20. The exact cost is always shown before you pay.

Can I return or exchange something?

Every sale is final, so have a look at the size guide first. If a piece turns up broken, faulty or not what you chose, write to us no later than 7 days after it arrives and we'll send a new one or refund you.

How do your pieces fit?

Most tops and hoodies have a relaxed fit with dropped shoulders. Order your usual size, or go up one size for a looser feel. Each product page lists its fit, and the size guide has full measurements.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, within 24 hours of placing it, as long as it hasn't started production. Email us with your order number and you'll receive a full refund to your original payment method.

Do you have a discount code?

Yes. New customers can use ABOVE10 for 10% off their first order. Enter it in your bag or at checkout. It applies to the merchandise subtotal only, one code per order, and can't be combined with other offers.
