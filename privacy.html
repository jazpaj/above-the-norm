Above The Norm LLC ("we", "us"), operates this website. This policy describes what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Order information: name, email, phone number, shipping and billing address, and the items you purchase.
  • Payment information: card details are entered directly into our payment processor's secure form and processed by them. We do not receive or store your full card number or security code.
  • Communications: anything you send us by email or through our contact form.
  • Device and usage data: basic information such as browser type, pages viewed and referring site, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your orders, including sending order confirmations and tracking.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you've opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the store, such as our payment processor, shipping carriers, website hosting and email providers, and only as needed for them to perform those services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies

We use essential cookies and browser storage to keep items in your bag and make checkout work. These are always on. With your permission, we may also use analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. When you first visit, a banner lets you choose "Accept all" or "Essential only", and your choice is saved in your browser. You can change it by clearing your browser's site data, or block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work without them.

Data security and retention

This site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and payments are handled by a PCI-DSS compliant processor. We keep order records for as long as needed for tax and accounting purposes, and other information only as long as it's useful for the purposes above.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you marketing emails, by emailing family@abovethenorm.us. Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
