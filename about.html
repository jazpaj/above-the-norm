About us

Above the norm, on purpose.

Above The Norm started in 2019 with one idea: the people who don't fit in are usually the ones worth paying attention to.

We make streetwear for outsiders, believers, late-night thinkers and anyone who's ever been told to just be normal. Our graphics pull from angels and aliens, old Hollywood, church flyers and back-yard UFO sightings, and turn them into pieces you'll want to wear until they fall apart.

How we make things

Every design is drawn in-house. We print in small runs on heavyweight blanks, with screen printing, puff print and embroidery, and add our own sewn-in labels. Producing in limited quantities means less waste, and it's why most orders ship within 1–2 weeks: your piece is finished for you, not pulled from a warehouse of overstock.

Who we are

Above The Norm is a registered trademark of Above The Norm LLC (U.S. Reg. No. 6,698,259). We're an independent, owner-operated brand: no investors, no licensing deals, just a small team and a community we call the #ATNFAMILY.
